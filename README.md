## Phase 2 — What Is Coming Next

### 1. SEO — Own "Pilates in Zimbabwe" on Google

Right now, anyone searching *"pilates in Harare"*, *"reformer pilates Zimbabwe"*, or *"pilates studio Harare"* is not finding Nirvana. We will fix that.

Here is exactly what we will do:

**Technical SEO**
- Add structured data (Schema.org `LocalBusiness` + `SportsActivityLocation`) so Google understands your studio is a physical, bookable location in Harare
- Set up a `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` so every page is indexed correctly
- Optimise page speed to score 90+ on Google Lighthouse — speed is a direct ranking factor
- Add canonical tags and Open Graph metadata so every shared link looks sharp on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook

**Local SEO**
- Set up and fully optimise your **Google Business Profile** (formerly Google My Business) — this is the single most powerful thing for local search; it puts you on the map, literally
- Add your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistently across every directory: Google, Facebook, Yelp, Foursquare, and Zimbabwe-specific business directories
- Build location-targeted landing pages: *"Reformer Pilates in Harare"*, *"Pilates Classes Borrowdale"*, *"Wellness Studio Zimbabwe"*
- Target long-tail keywords your competitors are not going after

**Content Strategy**
- A monthly blog cadence covering topics like *"Benefits of Reformer Pilates"*, *"How to Start Pilates in Zimbabwe"*, *"What to Expect at Your First Session"* — each article is a door that brings a new client to your site
- Alt text, meta descriptions, and title tags optimised on every image and page

**Goal:** Within 90 days, Nirvana Pilates ranks on page 1 for every relevant pilates and wellness search in Zimbabwe.

### 2. Admin Dashboard — Know Your Studio at a Glance

We will build a private, password-protected admin dashboard at `nirvana-pilates.vercel.app/admin` where you can log in and instantly see:

**Bookings Overview**
- Every upcoming session — who is coming, what service they booked, what time, and their contact details
- A colour-coded calendar view: confirmed (green), pending (amber), cancelled (red)
- Filter by service type, date range, or instructor

**Attendance Tracking**
- Mark clients as arrived, no-show, or rescheduled with one tap
- See attendance history per client — who is loyal, who dropped off, who might need a check-in
- Session capacity indicators so you never overbook

**Cancellation Intelligence**
- Automatic tracking of cancellations with timestamps and reasons
- 24-hour cancellation window enforcement — clients who cancel late are flagged
- A waitlist system that automatically moves the next client up when a slot opens

**Revenue Snapshot**
- At-a-glance monthly revenue total and per-service breakdown
- Spot which offerings are most popular and which need promotion

All of this in a clean, simple interface — no technical knowledge required.

### 3. WhatsApp Automation — Your Studio Runs Itself

Zimbabwe runs on WhatsApp. We will make it work for Nirvana around the clock.

**Booking Confirmations**
- The moment a client completes a booking on the website, they receive an automatic WhatsApp message confirming their session, the date, the time, and what to bring
- No manual follow-up required from your side

**Reminders**
- 24 hours before a session: *"Hi [Name], just a reminder that your Reformer Pilates session at Nirvana is tomorrow at 09:00. We look forward to seeing you."*
- 2 hours before: a final gentle nudge
- Reduce no-shows without a single staff member lifting a finger

**Cancellation Flow**
- If a client needs to cancel, they reply "CANCEL" to the WhatsApp message
- The system logs the cancellation, releases the slot, notifies you, and automatically messages the next person on the waitlist
- You receive a WhatsApp notification as the studio owner every time a cancellation happens

**Post-Session Follow-Up**
- 2 hours after their session: *"We hope you feel wonderful, [Name]. Would you like to book your next session?"* with a direct booking link
- 7 days later: a gentle re-engagement message if they have not rebooked
- This alone converts casual visitors into loyal regulars

**New Client Welcome**
- First-time clients receive a welcome sequence: what to wear, what to expect, how to prepare, and a warm personal note from the Nirvana team

**Integration**
- We use the official **WhatsApp Business API** (Meta-approved) — messages come from your verified Nirvana number, not a third-party number
- Fully integrated with the booking dashboard so everything is in one place
